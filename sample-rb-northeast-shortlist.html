I’d pick this if it were mine

If this dealer is buying to retail compact iron: go to North East, MD and look at the 2023 Case TV370B (catalog ID 15682081) first. 302 hours on a 2023 high-flow two-speed CTL with cab and an 84" bucket is the one that can still make a dealer whole after trucking and the buyer’s fee — if the belts, rollers, and hour meter survive a cold start and a walk-around. If you will not haul from Maryland: the 2019 Bobcat E85 in Burgettstown (15612070) is the Pennsylvania machine. Drive that first. 2,510 hours is the haircut. Thumb + blade + rubber is the reason it is on this list at all.

I would not bid any of these from the couch. Preview starts 12 Sep at North East, MD. Burgettstown is open weekdays now. Bidding opens 9 Sep — so the honest move is inspect before 9 Sep if you can, or inspect 12–14 Sep and bid with eyes, not hope.

Before you bid

  • Walk it. Start it cold. Cycle hydros, coupler, two-speed, A/C. Ritchie limited-function checks are not your inspection.
  • Hours vs wear. Painted tachs, belt wear that does not match 302 hours, or a door already gone — walk or bid junk money.
  • Serial on the plate = serial on the catalog. TV370B NPM425827. SV340B NMM402759. E85 B48412685.
  • Add buyer’s fee, trucking, and 23 Sep pay / 30 Sep removal. Late pickup gets storage; 45 days is abandoned under their terms.
  • Do not bid on unused Chinese mini-skids and “STARVOX / ARTEER / SDLOOL” lots unless that is your actual retail. This event is full of them.
