Shortlist of 3

Option 1 · cheap diesel

Yanmar YM1500D 4x4 diesel

$3,400 · Jamestown · 505 hours listed

Old Japanese compact. Small. If it is actually 4x4, 3-pt, and starts cold, this is the value play. 15 hp class. Fine for a few acres, a tiller, a small blade. Not a loader tractor.

Ask: hours real? 3-pt hitch on it? 4x4 engages? Smoke on start? Live PTO or transmission PTO?

Kioti LK3054 with loader, 30 hp

I’d pick this if it were mine

Look at the Yanmar $3,400 and the Kioti $8,900 the same afternoon. Both are Jamestown. That is one trip.

If the Yanmar is honest 4x4, 3-pt, 505 hours, and it fires cold, buy that and keep $5,000. If it is tired, smoky, or missing a hitch, walk and write a check for the Kioti — that is the actual compact with a loader.

Do not mail a deposit. See it run. Confirm hours, 3-pt, 4x4, cold start before cash.

Before you hand over cash

  • Start it cold. Wait for smoke to clear.
  • Count hours against wear. A painted-over tach is a walk-away.
  • Put it in 4x4. Lift the 3-pt. Cycle the loader if it has one.
  • Check for wet rear ends, wet loader joints, dry-rotted tires.
  • Title / bill of sale in the seller’s name. No stories.
